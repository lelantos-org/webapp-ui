The mark

A Merkle root and two leaves on a 32-unit grid. Nodes are r=4; the edges run at the angle between node centres, stroke 2.6, and stop 7.4u short of the root. Everything else follows from those three numbers.

Why this one

It is the structure the wallet actually keeps — tree-store.ts mirrors a Merkle tree locally. The tree is public; which leaf is yours is not. It reads as crypto on sight without a hexagon or a gradient, and it replaces the old icon on that icon's own terms: the same node triad, with the shield gone.

The wordmark

Martian Mono 600, +0.02em, uppercase. An instrumentation face — wide, squared, systematic — set at 0.62× the mark's height, with a gap of 0.44× between them. Barely used anywhere: #875 in the Google catalogue.
