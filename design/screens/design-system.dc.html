Five substitutions

v1's layout is unchanged. Every change below targets one of the traits that made it interchangeable with nine other DeFi apps.

Elementv1v2Reason
Display face Space Grotesk Newsreader Space Grotesk is a documented "slop font" and Namada's typeface — an adjacent shielded-pool project. Newsreader is a serif with an optical-size axis and tabular figures in the binary.
UI face system stack Public Sans The US Web Design System face, Franklin Gothic lineage. Institutional provenance, and deliberately unmemorable at 13px — which is what UI text should be.
Numerals JetBrains Mono Atkinson Mono JetBrains reads as IDE. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono was drawn by the Braille Institute to separate 0O 1lI 5S 8B — the exact failure mode of an address.
Ground #0f0f14 #14110E Every surveyed app sits in the #131313 blue-black family. A warm near-black is the same darkness in a hue nobody occupies.
Accent #7c74f5 #E0794A Blue-violet is the field's default. Serious privacy tools outside crypto run warm — Tailscale brick, Cloudflare orange, Mullvad yellow — and demote green and red to pure state semantics.
Radii 14px · 999px pills 4px · 2px The 12–24px family with pill chips is the single most recognisable DeFi tell. Near-zero is unoccupied and costs nothing.

Three devices added to the screens
The amount, twice

Figures and words, closed by a trailing rule so nothing can be appended. Cheques have done this for a century against the decimal-point slip — the characteristic catastrophic error here too. On Shield, Send and Unshield.

The warning band

A printed legend of what you can verify, in place of a padlock: decrypted on this device · checked against block 2,214,908 · no server was asked what you hold. Followed by the anti-boast — nobody here can see your balance, and that cuts both ways.

The observer view

On Unshield: exactly what the chain reveals. The only place a redaction bar appears — it shows what someone else is denied. Never your own figures from you.
