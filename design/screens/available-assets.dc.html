Four rules these surfaces must keep
A rate is never shown without its window 4.18% / yr · measured over 30d The registry ships the rate and the span as one field precisely so a consumer cannot show half of it. Thirty days of data is a different claim from a year of it.
Not measurable is not zero — An asset whose rate cannot be measured shows a dash. Rendering 0.00% would state that it earns nothing, which is a different and unsupported claim.
Paused still means backed paused · still fully backed A halted venue has degraded to plain custody. The balance is intact; it has only stopped growing. Never shown as an error.
Net of the pool’s cut already net The published figure has the protocol’s share removed, so no second deduction is implied anywhere in the UI.
The line not to cross Ranking these by rate, sorting highest-first, or badging one as “best” would turn a registry into a recommendation — and the wallet has no basis for one. The order is the registry’s own, held assets first. A rate is shown because it is a fact about a venue, not because the app has an opinion about where your money should go.
