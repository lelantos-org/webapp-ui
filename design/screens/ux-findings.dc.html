v2.1 — what still needs fixing UX, NOT LOOKS

The v2 reskin changed the surface and left the flows exactly as v1 had them. Eight problems survived. Two of them cost real money or a real proof; the rest cost trust. Every one is checked against the source, and where the code already gets something right, it says so.

01 Claim links are silently evicted at 50 records — this loses funds Can lose funds

link-vault.ts caps the store at MAX_RECORDS = 50 and keeps the newest. The 51st link drops the oldest record, and that record is the spending key. The file says what follows: Once lost, the funds sit at an ephemeral address with no recoverable key. There is a 30-day TTL too, and the store is browser-local.

FIX Show capacity before it bites. Warn on eviction instead of doing it silently. Offer an export.
02 An unaffordable fee asset lets you submit, then fails after a full proof High

The picker refuses an unaffordable asset correctly — but the default is never checked (payingWith = feeAsset ?? selected?.id) and affordable appears nowhere in the submit gate. Never open the picker and you pay a whole prepare-and-prove cycle to be told Insufficient balance for this amount., which does not name the fee asset.

FIX Resolve the fee asset up front, show it in the closed Details row, and refuse before the proof — naming the asset.
03 Irreversible sends have no review step High

Send and Unshield go from form straight to a filled button. The transfer cannot be reversed, cancelled, or confirmed by the recipient. The claim-link generator gates itself behind a checkbox because its authors judged that risk worth a pause — a transfer is no less final.

FIX A review step: figures and words, destination in readable groups, resolved fees, the boundary named, one deliberate confirm.
04 “Needs consolidating” names a remedy the user cannot perform High

The wallet does explain why Max sits under the balance — balance-hint.ts renders 42 WETH needs consolidating, because the circuit spends at most 4 notes. But the only control that sounds like the remedy is Compact, whose own copy says Balances are unchanged. Real consolidation is autoConsolidate: true, hardcoded and invisible.

FIX Say what the user can actually do — name the automatic behaviour, or offer consolidation explicitly with its cost. Stop “compact” implying it.
05 A syncing wallet is indistinguishable from an empty one Medium

Balances exist only after syncNotes completes, and a cold sync can run for minutes. The forms stay live with zero balances, so input is rejected as insufficient funds. SyncErrorNotice states this hazard exactly — but renders nothing while a sync is succeeding, so it covers the error case, not the slow one every new user hits.

FIX Extend the notice to slow success. Hold the forms in an explicit “still adding up your balance” state instead of letting them read as empty.
06 Transfer and Withdraw disable submit without saying why Medium

The swap form does this well — an ordered reason table with a stated rationale. Transfer and Withdraw pass submitDisabled={!v.valid} with no reason string at all, and the recipient is not gated: a malformed address is caught only after the click.

FIX Give both the swap form's treatment, and gate the recipient.
07 The claim-link form never reads your balance Medium

Stated outright in GenerateLinkForm.tsx: this form does not read the sender's shielded balance, so insufficient is not something it can determine. You can request a link for more than you hold and find out at submit.

FIX Read the balance, or say plainly that the amount is unchecked until submit.
08 The mobile set stops after two screens Medium

Only Home and Shield are drawn. Send, Unshield, Swap and Claim are desktop-only — and Claim is the most likely of all to be opened on a phone, because it arrives as a shared link.

FIX Draw the remaining four at 390×844.

No activity or history, anywhere

Six routes, all of them actions. Nothing on Home. The only trace of a completed send is a tx hash in React state — cleared on the next interaction and lost on reload. You send money and it leaves the interface. This is the largest single gap in the product.

No address book

Zero matches in the codebase for contacts or recent recipients. Every send needs a long shielded address obtained out of band and pasted, every time — and the field is cleared on submit and destroyed on a chain switch.
